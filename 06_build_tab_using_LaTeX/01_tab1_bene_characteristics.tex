\footnotesize
\noindent
\textit{Notes:} Both samples are restricted to beneficiaries with chronic kidney disease. Stars are shown on the early dialysis column only and indicate tests of differences between the early and non-early dialysis groups: * \(p < 0.10\), ** \(p < 0.05\), *** \(p < 0.01\). Continuous variables are compared using two-sample t-tests while proportions are compared using two-sample tests of proportions.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{a} The hurricane wind-exposed sample includes beneficiaries residing in counties estimated to have been exposed to wind speeds of at least 17 m/s from the hurricane.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{b} The facility-disruption sample includes beneficiaries treated at facilities with hurricane-associated disruptions. A facility was classified as disrupted when, during a given week associated with hurricane exposure, at least one-third of the beneficiaries it served received one or fewer dialysis sessions.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{c} The early dialysis group includes beneficiaries where the last dialysis session before the hurricane exposure date occurred on an off-schedule day rather than on the patient's usual 3-day dialysis schedule. The hurricane exposure date is defined by the timestamp of when the eye of the storm was closest to the county centroid of where the beneficiaries lived.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{d} \(N\) denotes the number of beneficiary--storm observations.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{e} Comorbidity score is the combined Elixhauser--Charlson comorbidity score, constructed using diagnoses observed within the year on and prior to hurricane exposure.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{f} ESRD primary Medicare eligibility indicates that end-stage renal disease (ESRD) was the primary basis for Medicare entitlement rather than age or disability.
\\[0.35em]
\tn{g} Maximum estimated sustained wind speed is measured in meters per second (m/s).
\\[0.35em]
\tn{h} Metropolitan counties have at least one urbanized area with a population of at least 50{,}000; micropolitan counties have at least one urban cluster with a population between 10{,}000 and 50{,}000.
\\[0.35em]
Abbreviations: AMI, acute myocardial infarction; CHF, congestive heart failure; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; ESRD, end-stage renal disease; TIA, transient ischemic attack; hosp, hospital; pop, population.
