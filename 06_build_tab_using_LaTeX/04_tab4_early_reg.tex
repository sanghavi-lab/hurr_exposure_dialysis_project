\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]
\footnotesize

\item[]
Notes: The analysis calculates the within-beneficiary percentage-point difference from refer-
ence to hazard week among the early dialysis group relative to the non-early dialysis group.
The early dialysis group includes beneficiaries where the last dialysis session before the hur-
ricane exposure date occurred on an off-schedule day rather than on the patient’s usual
3-day dialysis schedule.

\item[a]
Obs $N$ is the number of paired beneficiary–storm observations with both the reference
week (two weeks before storm exposure) and the exposure week.

\item[b]
A positive coefficient indicates worse outcomes among the early dialysis group relative to
the non-early dialysis group, while a negative coefficient indicates better outcomes. Standard
errors are two-way clustered at the facility and storm levels.

\item[c]
The hurricane wind-exposed sample includes beneficiaries residing in counties estimated to
have been exposed to wind speeds of at least 17 m/s from the hurricane.

\item[d]
The facility-disruption sample includes beneficiaries treated at facilities with hurricane-
associated disruptions. A facility was classified as disrupted when, during a given week
associated with hurricane exposure, at least one-third of the beneficiaries it served received
one or fewer dialysis sessions.

\item[e]
The post-exposure period for the disruption outcome is relative days 1-7 instead of days 0-6 as defined for outcomes ED, IP, and Mortality.

\end{tablenotes}
