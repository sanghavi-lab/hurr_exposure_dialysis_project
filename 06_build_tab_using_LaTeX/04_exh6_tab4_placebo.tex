\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]
\footnotesize

\item[] Panel A notes: The placebo exposure week (week $-5$) is defined as 5 weeks before the exposure start date. The placebo reference week corresponds to two weeks prior (week $-7$) to the placebo exposure week. The first row compares the placebo reference week with the placebo exposure week. The second through fourth rows compare the placebo reference week with cumulative outcome windows through weeks $-5$ to $-4$,  $-5$ to $-3$, and  $-5$ to $-2$.

\addlinespace[0.8em]
\item[] Panel B notes: This test assesses whether week $-2$ is a stable reference period by comparing earlier pre-period weeks $-6$ through $-3$ with week $-2$ within beneficiaries. Mortality is evaluated in Panel A only and is not estimated in Panel B.

\addlinespace[0.8em]
\item[a] The hurricane wind-exposed sample includes beneficiaries residing in counties estimated to have been exposed to wind speeds of at least 17 m/s from the hurricane.

\item[b] Obs $N$ is the number of paired beneficiary--storm observations. In Panel A, a pair is a beneficiary--storm observation with both week $-7$ and $-5$ present. In Panel B, a pair is a beneficiary--storm observation with both week $-2$ and the comparison pre-period week present.

\item[c] Effects are reported in percentage points. In Panel A, effects are within-beneficiary percentage-point differences between the placebo exposure week and the placebo reference week. In Panel B, effects are within-beneficiary percentage-point differences between week $k$ and week $-2$, where $k \in \{-6,-5,-4,-3\}$. Standard errors are two-way clustered at the facility and storm levels in both panels.

\end{tablenotes}
